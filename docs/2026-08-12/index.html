NRC holds public meeting for proposed rule eliminating ALARA

The Nuclear Regulatory Commission has proposed a change to how it will approach standards for radiation protections, notably including the elimination of the “as low as reasonably achievable” (ALARA) principle. The proposed change was published in the Federal Register on July 15, and on Monday the agency held a public meeting on the topic. Go deeper on Nuclear Newswire.

NRC shares more on reactor licensing proposed rule at virtual Q&A

The Nuclear Regulatory Commission’s proposed rule on modernizing reactor licensing, safety oversight, and siting practices ran over 550 pages when it was unveiled in July. To address the massive rulemaking, the NRC held three meetings last week to address all the topics. Go deeper on Nuclear Newswire.

Calif. officials urge Diablo Canyon extension, but Newsom holding off

Dozens of elected officials in California are urging Gov. Gavin Newsom and the California Legislature to act this year to extend operations at Diablo Canyon nuclear power plant past 2030. Whether state lawmakers will take action on this remains to be seen, especially as recent comments from Newsom seem to shift this responsibility to the next governor, who will take office in 2027. Go deeper on Nuclear Newswire.

Diversification and the common ground

“Who would have thought, just a few years ago, that we would see so many long-standing barriers to new nuclear development falling like dominoes? Public opinion, policy, regulatory reform, finance and investment, design maturity, nuclear fuel enrichment, and fuel fabrication capacity have all advanced with remarkable speed in the United States.” Read ANS CEO Craig Piercy’s full column on Nuclear Newswire.
